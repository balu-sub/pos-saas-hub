MODULE 1 • STARTING SCREEN & COUNTER CHECKOUT

1. First Screen & POS Billing Menu

When you open the Plannest POS application on your billing counter computer, this is the very first screen you see. It is designed so any cashier can scan items, weigh vegetables/fruits, and print a customer bill in seconds.

1.1 Understanding the First Screen (Top Bar, Left Menu, Product Grid & Bottom Bar)

ID: help-sec-01-01-first-screen
🖥
LOCAL COUNTER APP SCREEN This fast billing screen appears on your store counter computer. It works 100% offline even if your internet cable is unplugged.
What is this screen for? This is your main billing counter screen. It is divided into 4 simple areas: the Top Header Bar (store info, live scale weight, language, cashier name), the Left Navigation Menu (to switch between the 6 store modules), the Center Product Catalog (all your store items with photos and prices), and the Right Shopping Basket (the current customer's bill).

📋 What Data & Controls Are Available on This First Screen?

Screen Area Button / Item Name What Data It Shows & What It Does
Top Header (Left) ☰ Menu Toggle & Store Name Shows your store name (e.g., Amma Appa Kadai). Clicking ☰ (or pressing Ctrl+B) hides the left menu to make the product screen wider.
Top Header (Center) 0.000 kg Live Scale Display Shows the live weight sitting on your electronic weighing machine. Clicking it resets (Zero/Tare) the scale to 0.000 kg.
Top Header (Right) EN / தமிழ் / हिन्दी & Cashier Profile Changes item names between English, Tamil, and Hindi immediately. Next to it is the live clock and the logged-in Cashier/Admin name (click to switch cashier using a 4-digit PIN).
Left Sidebar Menu 6 Main Menus + Help / Guide + Lock / Exit Lets you switch between POS Billing, Admin Hub, Cash & UPI, Supplier & Replenishment, Store Sales Analytics, and Peripherals & Hardware. Click « to shrink the menu to icons only.
Center Catalog Area Search Bar, OFFERS Strip & Category Tabs Shows all active products (e.g., All Items (170), Fruits, Vegetables, Dairy, Groceries). Each product card displays its Photo, Category, 4-digit SKU code (e.g., 0095), Name, MRP struck through, Selling Price, and discount badge (e.g., 13% OFF).
Bottom Status Strip Online Status, Reload DB, Touch ON, Kiosk Shows green Online dot, total items synced, how many hardware peripherals are active, a Touch ON button for on-screen touch keyboard, and Kiosk mode.

1.2 Adding Items to the Shopping Basket & Adjusting Quantities

ID: help-sec-01-02-active-cart
What is this for? As a customer brings items to the counter, you add them to the right-side Shopping Basket by scanning barcodes, clicking product cards, or clicking the top daily offer buttons.

👉 Step-by-Step: How to Add & Edit Items in a Bill

1
Scan or Tap an Item: Point your barcode scanner at the packet, OR tap any product picture on the screen (for example, Alphonso or Arokya Green), OR type the item name/Tamil name in the top search box.
2
Change Quantity: In the right-side basket, tap + or - to increase or decrease the count, or click inside the white number box to type an exact weight (like 0.750 for 750 grams).
3
Remove an Item or Clear Basket: Click the × on the right side of any item row to remove just that item, or click Clear All at the bottom to empty the entire basket.
4
Hold / Park a Bill: If a customer forgets their wallet or goes back to pick another item, click Hold / Park. Their basket is saved safely under Held at the top right so you can bill the next person in line immediately!

1.3 Numpad SKU Quick Entry Modal (F2)

ID: help-sec-01-03-numpad-sku
What is this for? Many loose items (like coriander, ginger, or loose grains) do not have a barcode sticker. Every item in your store has a short 4-digit SKU code (shown in a blue box on every product card, such as 0095). Clicking Numpad SKU [F2] lets you punch in the number and weight using big touch buttons.
1
Click Numpad SKU [F2] at the top of the billing screen (or press F2 on your keyboard).
2
Type the item's SKU number (e.g., 0095) or Barcode, enter the Quantity/Weight, and click Add to Bill.

1.4 Duplicate Bill / Search Past Receipts Modal (F7)

ID: help-sec-01-04-duplicate-bill
What is this for? If a customer asks for an extra copy of their bill or comes back to verify an old bill, click Duplicate Bill [F7] to look up past bills and reprint them in one click.
1
Click Duplicate Bill [F7] next to the search bar.
2
Type the Bill Number (e.g., INV/1001) or Customer Name/Phone, select the bill from the list, and click Reprint Receipt.

1.5 Customer Selection, Loyalty Points & Khata Store Credit

ID: help-sec-01-05-customer-khata
What is this for? At the top of the right-side basket, the default selection is Walk-in Retail Customer. Clicking it lets you attach a regular customer to the bill so they earn Loyalty Points or use their Khata (Store Credit) account.
1
Click Walk-in Retail Customer ▾ at the top right of the screen.
2
Search by the customer's phone number or name and click their name. Their available Loyalty Points and Khata Credit Balance will appear automatically.
3
If they are a new regular customer, click + Add New Customer inside the dropdown, enter their Name and Mobile Number, and click Save.

1.6 Payment & Split-Tender Checkout Modal (F12)

ID: help-sec-01-06-checkout-payment
What is this for? Once all items are in the basket, clicking PROCEED TO PAY [F12] opens the Payment window. Customers can pay the full amount by UPI / QR, Cash INR, Card (EDC), Khata Credit, or Redeem Loyalty Points—or even split a single bill across two methods (for example, Rs. 500 in Cash + rest by UPI)!

👉 Step-by-Step: How to Complete a Sale & Print the Bill

1
Click PROCEED TO PAY [F12] (or press F12 on your keyboard).
2
Choose Payment Method: Click UPI / QR, Cash INR, Card (EDC), or Khata Credit.
3
Add Payment Amount: If paying full amount, click + Add Split (+ Rs. ...) on the right side (or tap currency note buttons like + ₹500 to calculate change to return to the customer).
4
Print Receipt: Once Balance Remaining shows Rs. 0.00, click COMPLETE SALE & PRINT RECEIPT. The thermal printer will immediately print the bill and the basket will clear for the next customer.
MODULE 2 • INVENTORY, FEFO BATCHES & SPOILAGE (LOCAL & WEB PARITY)

2. Inventory Menu (3 Submenus)

The dedicated Inventory menu centralizes all cataloguing, item pricing, First-Expired-First-Out (FEFO) batch tracking, and perishable spoilage/wastage recording. It is identical across both the Counter POS Terminal and the Cloud Web Manager.

2.1 Submenu 1: Catalogue & Stock Master

ID: help-sec-02-01-catalog-stock
🌐💻
AVAILABLE IN BOTH LOCAL APP & WEB CLOUD MANAGER All catalog updates, price modifications, stock adjustments, and product additions synchronize seamlessly between your billing counter and web manager.
What is this submenu for? Manage your complete master catalog of goods (e.g., 200+ produce, grocery, and packaged items). Here you can edit retail selling prices, purchase costs, barcode assignments, stock-on-hand, HSN codes, GST tax brackets (0%, 5%, 12%, 18%), and export data to CSV.
🖼️ Dual Image Storage Architecture:
• Cloudinary Global CDN: Products selected from the 170+ Cloud Catalog use global Cloudinary CDN links (https://res.cloudinary.com/...) which display across both the Cloud Manager and all POS terminals.
• Local Terminal Drive Storage: Custom product photos captured or added directly at counter terminals are stored on the counter machine's local drive (/images/products/...). In the Cloud Web App, these are marked with an "Image in Local POS" badge and operate offline without requiring external web hosting.
🏷️ Controlled Special Discounts: When configuring item deals, use the "Enable Special Discount" checkbox. Only items with this setting enabled will display the special discount option in the POS checkout cart.

📋 Catalogue Data Columns & Functions

Column / Button Data Displayed Operational Usage
Item Name & Tamil Name Official bilingual display name (e.g., Tomato Hybrid / தக்காளி). Appears on billing search, customer receipt, and barcode sticker labels.
SKU & Barcode System-assigned sequential SKU (e.g., SKU-0012) and 13-digit EAN/UPC barcode. Scanned by laser barcode readers or punched into Numpad [F2].
Category & Unit Merchandise group (Vegetables, Fruits, Dairy, Groceries) and measurement unit (kg, g, pc, pack, dozen). Determines whether item connects to weighing scale (kg/g) or quantity counter (pc/pack).
Purchase Cost vs Selling Price Wholesale buy rate (₹) and counter retail selling price (₹). Used for live gross margin calculations and automatically applies promotional discounts.
Stock On Hand & Status Current inventory level (e.g., 45.500 kg) with low stock alert indicator. Decrements automatically on every finalized receipt and increments on purchase inward.
Actions (Edit, Deal, Barcode) Interactive row actions to modify prices, launch clearance promotions, or print sticker labels. Allows 1-click price updates and label generation for counter display.

2.2 Submenu 2: Batch & Expiry Date Management (FEFO)

ID: help-sec-02-02-batch-expiry
🌐💻
FIRST-EXPIRED FIRST-OUT (FEFO) ENGINE Enforces strict FEFO compliance to eliminate dead stock, expired food hazards, and shrinkage losses.
What is this submenu for? Tracks individual inward consignments by Batch Number, Manufacturing Date (MFG), and Expiry Date (EXP). During counter billing, Plannest automatically allocates stock from the earliest expiring batch, ensuring older inventory is sold first.

🪓 4-Tier Real-Time Expiry Status KPI Cards

KPI Status Bracket Days to Expiration Visual Badge Color Recommended Counter Action
Expired Batches Past Expiry Date (≤ 0 days) ⚠ Expired Immediately remove from retail floor. Click Discard Spoilage to write off to the waste ledger.
Critical Expiry 1 to 7 Days Remaining ⏰ < 7 Days Critical Launch clearance deal or 30%-50% discount to clear inventory before expiration.
Near Expiry 8 to 30 Days Remaining ⚠ 8-30 Days Near Expiry Place front-and-center on shelves and highlight on daily promotional displays.
Healthy Stock More than 30 Days Remaining ✓ Healthy Standard shelf rotation; regular stock monitoring.

👉 Step-by-Step: Registering a New Inward Batch

1
Click + Inward New Batch at the top right of the Batch & Expiry screen.
2
Select the target product from your catalog (e.g., Arokya Toned Milk 500ml).
3
Enter the supplier's Batch / Lot Number (e.g., LOT-2026-11A), Inward Quantity, Cost Price, and Expiry Date.
4
Click Confirm Batch Inward. The batch immediately registers in the FEFO allocation queue and syncs to both local terminal and cloud database!

2.3 Submenu 3: Wastage, Spoilage & Produce Shrinkage Tracking

ID: help-sec-02-03-wastage-spoilage
🌐💻
AUDIT COMPLIANT PERISHABLE WRITE-OFF LEDGER Provides an irrefutable audit trail for perishable shrinkage, damaged produce, and expired items.
What is this submenu for? In retail produce and grocery, items spoil, rot, or get damaged during handling. Recording disposals here deducts the exact physical weight (kg) or count from inventory balances, logs the financial loss at cost price, and creates an audit record stamped with operator name and timestamp.

🗑 Standardized Loss Reason Classifications

Loss Reason Code Typical Grocery & Produce Scenarios Inventory Ledger Impact
Rotten / Spoiled Perishable Fresh vegetables, leafy greens, or fruits that softened, decayed, or developed mold on display. Deducted from stock; loss calculated as Quantity × Cost Price.
Damaged in Transit / Inward Bruised boxes, leaking milk sachets, or broken eggs during supplier delivery. Logged for supplier credit note debit claim or written off.
Shelf Life Expired Packaged dairy, bakery, or beverages exceeding best-before date before sale. Deducted from batch inventory; recorded under expiry losses.
Handling Shrinkage / Drying Natural moisture loss and desiccation in root vegetables, onions, and potatoes. Stock balance adjusted to match actual physical weigh-in scale readings.
Customer Dropped / Spilled Glass jar breakage, dropped bottles, or torn flour bags on counter. Written off under accidental breakage expense.

📝 Printed Bill Font Sizing & Thermal Paper Formatting (v2.4.2)

Plannest POS features real-time selectable font scaling on printed customer receipts without needing driver tweaks. Directly from the Live Bill Preview panel, users can toggle between three calibrated font sizes:

  • 1x (Default): Standard compact receipt typography suitable for high item counts.
  • 1.5x (Large): Enhanced readability for senior customers or low-light retail counters.
  • 1.75x (Extra): High-visibility bold printing for customer clarity, calibrated so text never bleeds past standard 76mm/80mm roll boundaries.
Anti-Bleed Safeguard: At 1.5x and 1.75x font sizes, item names wrap cleanly across multiple lines without truncating words, and redundant currency signs on line item rows are streamlined to guarantee zero horizontal margin overflow. The preview panel is independently scrollable with pinned controls at the top.

3.4 Automated Rolling Backups & SuperAdmin Disaster Recovery

ID: help-sec-03-04-disaster-recovery
🛡️
ZERO CLUTTER • FULLY AUTOMATED CLOUD DISASTER RECOVERY No manual backup buttons to remember. Backups run automatically in the background every night, while disaster restoration is strictly authorized by SuperAdmin directive for tamper-proof safety.
How does Disaster Recovery work? If a counter PC crashes, corrupts, or gets accidentally formatted:
1. Store owner contacts Plannest SuperAdmin via verified phone.
2. SuperAdmin authorizes the recovery directive in the Plannest Master SaaS Hub.
3. Store owner simply clicks "Reload DB" in the POS footer. The terminal instantly pulls down the complete catalog, customer accounts, batches, and Khata ledgers!

Card / Area What Data It Shows
