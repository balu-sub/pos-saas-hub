MODULE 1 • STARTING SCREEN & COUNTER CHECKOUT

1. First Screen & POS Billing Menu

When you open the Plannest POS application on your billing counter computer, this is the very first screen you see. It is designed so any cashier can scan items, weigh vegetables/fruits, and print a customer bill in seconds.

1.1 Understanding the First Screen (Top Bar, Left Menu, Product Grid & Bottom Bar)

ID: help-sec-01-01-first-screen
🖥
LOCAL COUNTER APP SCREEN This fast billing screen appears on your store counter computer. It works 100% offline even if your internet cable is unplugged.
What is this screen for? This is your main billing counter screen. It is divided into 4 simple areas: the Top Header Bar (store info, live scale weight, language, cashier name), the Left Navigation Menu (to switch between the 6 store modules), the Center Product Catalog (all your store items with photos and prices), and the Right Shopping Basket (the current customer's bill).

📋 What Data & Controls Are Available on This First Screen?

Screen Area Button / Item Name What Data It Shows & What It Does
Top Header (Left) ☰ Menu Toggle & Store Name Shows your store name (e.g., Amma Appa Kadai). Clicking ☰ (or pressing Ctrl+B) hides the left menu to make the product screen wider.
Top Header (Center) 0.000 kg Live Scale Display Shows the live weight sitting on your electronic weighing machine. Clicking it resets (Zero/Tare) the scale to 0.000 kg.
Top Header (Right) EN / தமிழ் / हिन्दी & Cashier Profile Changes item names between English, Tamil, and Hindi immediately. Next to it is the live clock and the logged-in Cashier/Admin name (click to switch cashier using a 4-digit PIN).
Left Sidebar Menu 6 Main Menus + Help / Guide + Lock / Exit Lets you switch between POS Billing, Admin Hub, Cash & UPI, Supplier & Replenishment, Store Sales Analytics, and Peripherals & Hardware. Click « to shrink the menu to icons only.

Each product card displays its Photo, Category, 4-digit SKU code (e.g., 0095), Name, MRP struck through, Selling Price, and discount badge (e.g., 13% OFF).
Bottom Status Strip Online Status, Reload DB, Touch ON, Kiosk Shows green Online dot, total items synced, how many hardware peripherals are active, a Touch ON button for on-screen touch keyboard, and Kiosk mode.

1.2 Adding Items to the Shopping Basket & Adjusting Quantities

ID: help-sec-01-02-active-cart
What is this for? As a customer brings items to the counter, you add them to the right-side Shopping Basket by scanning barcodes, clicking product cards, or clicking the top daily offer buttons.

👉 Step-by-Step: How to Add & Edit Items in a Bill

1
Scan or Tap an Item: Point your barcode scanner at the packet, OR tap any product picture on the screen (for example, Alphonso or Arokya Green), OR type the item name/Tamil name in the top search box.
2
Change Quantity: In the right-side basket, tap + or - to increase or decrease the count, or click inside the white number box to type an exact weight (like 0.750 for 750 grams).
3
Remove an Item or Clear Basket: Click the × on the right side of any item row to remove just that item, or click Clear All at the bottom to empty the entire basket.
4
Hold / Park a Bill: If a customer forgets their wallet or goes back to pick another item, click Hold / Park. Their basket is saved safely under Held at the top right so you can bill the next person in line immediately!

1.3 Numpad SKU Quick Entry Modal (F2)

ID: help-sec-01-03-numpad-sku

Every item in your store has a short 4-digit SKU code (shown in a blue box on every product card, such as 0095). Clicking Numpad SKU [F2] lets you punch in the number and weight using big touch buttons.

2
Type the item's SKU number (e.g., 0095) or Barcode, enter the Quantity/Weight, and click Add to Bill.

2
Type the Bill Number (e.g., INV/1001) or Customer Name/Phone, select the bill from the list, and click Reprint Receipt.

ID: help-sec-01-05-customer-khata

1.6 Payment & Split-Tender Checkout Modal (F12)

ID: help-sec-01-06-checkout-payment
What is this for? Once all items are in the basket, clicking PROCEED TO PAY [F12] opens the Payment window. Customers can pay the full amount by UPI / QR, Cash INR, Card (EDC), Khata Credit, or Redeem Loyalty Points—or even split a single bill across two methods (for example, Rs. 500 in Cash + rest by UPI)!

👉 Step-by-Step: How to Complete a Sale & Print the Bill

1
Click PROCEED TO PAY [F12] (or press F12 on your keyboard).
2
Choose Payment Method: Click UPI / QR, Cash INR, Card (EDC), or Khata Credit.
3
Add Payment Amount: If paying full amount, click + Add Split (+ Rs. ...) on the right side (or tap currency note buttons like + ₹500 to calculate change to return to the customer).
4
Print Receipt: Once Balance Remaining shows Rs. 0.00, click COMPLETE SALE & PRINT RECEIPT. The thermal printer will immediately print the bill and the basket will clear for the next customer.
